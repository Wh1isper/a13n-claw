# Execution Lifecycle

## Entry and Observation

Claw routes retained external input or passes an explicitly targeted human operation to Service. Direct human submission follows Service admission; it is not a second product prompt queue. Claw links accepted Entries and Runs back to their originating work. While compatible work is active, new input may steer it; otherwise it remains queued or advances separately under Service policy. A native wait is continued only by a valid response. Execution receipts, saved output, and business settlement are separate facts.

IM and operator clients present accepted work, progress, saved outcomes, and failure. A disconnected client reloads authoritative state rather than resubmitting a prompt. A submission whose response was lost is reconciled against the original request; an uncertain effect is not reproduced using a new identity.

## Decisions and Controls

An authorized operator can inspect work, steer it, answer a native question or approval, and stop the selected operation. Decisions target the exact waiting Run and pending requests, preserve the native response kinds and complete batch semantics, and can be answered through supported IM interaction or headless operations. A stale wait or control cannot apply to replacement work. A shared conversation does not grant every participant permission to inspect, stop, or approve its work.

A retained tool execution has its own identity and control independent of a Run. Cancellation targets the selected Run or retained tool; it does not undo external effects or settle a business Inbox item. [One Thread mode](10-one-thread-mode.md) owns that separate settlement.
